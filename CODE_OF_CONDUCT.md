# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Participants are expected to be respectful, direct, and constructive. Good-faith disagreement is welcome; harassment, personal attacks, and abusive behavior are not.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block participants whose behavior is harmful to the project or its users.

## Scope

This applies to project spaces including issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, and related community channels.
